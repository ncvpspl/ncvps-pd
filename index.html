CRAFT Prompt Challenge: Seeding Level

Your mission: Inspect realistic AI prompts an NCVPS teacher might use. At the Seeding level, you are not trying to write the perfect prompt from scratch. You are learning to notice what makes a prompt more useful, more specific, and easier to act on.

Accessibility note: Every interaction works with a keyboard, feedback is written in text rather than communicated by color alone, and no timed responses are required.

CHALLENGE 1

Announcement Upgrade

NCVPS situation: You want AI to help draft a weekly announcement. The course content and assignments already exist.

Teacher Prompt:
"Write an announcement reminding students to complete this week's work."
Which addition would most improve the Context of this prompt?
Seeding clue

Context gives AI the situation and background it needs. In NCVPS, that often means the module, current pacing point, deadlines, or what students should pay attention to, not creating new course content.

CHALLENGE 2

Prompt Inspector

NCVPS situation: A student has stopped submitting work, and you want help drafting a supportive outreach message.

Teacher Prompt:
"Act as a supportive online teacher. Write a short message to a high school student who has not submitted work this week. Ask the student to reply with one barrier that is getting in the way and one next step they can take. Keep the tone encouraging and nonjudgmental. Format the message as a short Canvas Inbox message."
Which CRAFT elements are clearly present? Select all that apply.
Why this is useful

Strong prompts do not always need every CRAFT element to be equally detailed. Seeding practice is about recognizing what is already doing useful work before adding more.

CHALLENGE 3

Accommodation-Aware Prompt

NCVPS situation: You want AI to help make a teacher-created weekly checklist easier to follow for a student who benefits from chunked directions. You are not asking AI to change course requirements.

Teacher Prompt:
"Make this easier for my student with an IEP."
Which revision gives AI the clearest and safest direction?
Seeding clue

Look for prompts that tell AI what support to provide while preserving the original course expectations. The prompt should describe the needed adjustment rather than asking AI to determine a student's services.

CHALLENGE 4

Which Prompt Is More Usable?

NCVPS situation: You need a concise parent or guardian communication about missing work.

Prompt A
"Write an email about missing assignments."
Prompt B
"Write a brief email to a parent or guardian explaining that the student currently has three missing assignments. Use a professional, supportive tone. Include a clear invitation to contact me with questions and a reminder that the student can still take a next step. Keep the message under 150 words and include a subject line."
Which prompt is more likely to produce a message you can use with minimal editing?
CRAFT scan

Prompt B identifies the audience, task, tone, and format, while also providing enough situational context to guide the message. It is specific without asking AI to invent course content.

FINAL CHALLENGE

The CRAFT Boss: Best-Fit Prompt

NCVPS situation: A student has logged in but has not meaningfully engaged with the course. You want help drafting outreach that encourages a response without sounding punitive.

Which prompt best uses CRAFT for this situation?
What to notice after you answer

The strongest prompt gives AI a role, audience, situation, message format, specific task, and desired tone. It also places a useful boundary around the output by telling AI not to invent assignment details.
